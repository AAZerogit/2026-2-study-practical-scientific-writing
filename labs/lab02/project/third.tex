\section{Special characters}
These commands print special characters:
\{ \} \$ \% \& \# \_ \textbackslash{} \textasciicircum{} \textasciitilde{}.

Examples: a 15\% discount, a price of 20\$, the filename
\texttt{my\_file.tex}, item \#1, and \texttt{A\&B}.

\section{Mathematics}
An inline formula: \(a^2+b^2=c^2\).
A numbered equation:
\begin{equation}
  \label{eq:quadratic}
  x_{1,2}=\frac{-b\pm\sqrt{b^2-4ac}}{2a}, \qquad a\ne 0.
\end{equation}
A reference to the equation: \eqref{eq:quadratic}.
